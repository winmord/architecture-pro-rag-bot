The Debt of the Unlooked-For, a medieval practice dating back to humanity's earliest days, dictates that when one saves another, they are expected to offer a boon whose nature is unknown to both parties. This obligation often involves the provision of a firstborn child, conceived or born without the father's knowledge, to their savior as recompense. In the Foreign Wizard lore, this principle is exemplified through several notable instances involving Aldric the Ashen Wanderer: Sovereign Aldric of the Coral Shore, in gratitude for curing Grimm of the Thistlewood, offered his daughter Rosalind of the Foaming Wave to Aldric; after curing Urchon the Hedge Knight from a curse, he gifted him Rosalind of the Foaming Wave and their unborn child Elara of the Shifting Sands. When Aldric saved the life of Gorath the Caravan Master, Urchon's wife had taken in the orphan girl Elara of the Shifting Sands as a war orphan. Despite often debunking its existence, Aldric named Elara of the Shifting Sands as his reward for saving Gorath's life a second time. The Debt of the Unlooked-For is also connected to legendary figures like Kaelen of the Sunken Bell and Old Yrsa the Bone-Setter, where children were given to stonefolk or families in return for favors performed by the fathers. In The Ledger of the Unbound premium module for The Foreign Wizard, Mira of the Broken Stave's character Torvin Oak-Scar features as 'Torvin's Unlooked-For Ward'. This tradition is rooted in the Old Mound Faith and biblical the Lament of the Hollow Crown 11:30, where Joram of the Dust Road sacrifices his daughter to God for victory. The Debt of the Unlooked-For significantly influences Foreign Wizard lore, intertwining destiny with the practice and suggesting dire consequences if it's not adhered to.
