The Second Bloodshed at Ashenford, also known as the Second Bloodshed at Ashenford, was a significant conflict during the First March of the Iron Dominion, taking place in Lower Ashenford. This engagement involved twenty-two mages from various factions including the Crownwild Compact, Thornwood Host, Stormwatch Legion, Northmarch Banner, Greywater Phalanx, the Ashen Conclave, Ironbreakers, and several other mages. The hill, referred to as 'The Weavers' Knoll', 'The Mournful Rise', or 'The Hill of the Silent Quorum' due to fourteen mage deaths, is notable for thirteen confirmed casualties and one hundred thousand participants, with at least thirty thousand killed or wounded. Marshal Blackthorn's disastrous defeat marked this battle, contrary to previous victories. The exact number of bodies buried on the hill is debated, but it includes Rowena of the Copper Grove and Morwen of the Obsidian Falls, though only Sanna of the Mourning Veil from the fourteen was found among them. Each year at The Lanterns' Eve, fires are lit to commemorate the fallen mages. The battlefield, as described in Elara of the Shifting Sands's visions, is depicted as a vast sea of grass overlooking undulating fields covered by lush grass and flat-topped hills with mage stands.
