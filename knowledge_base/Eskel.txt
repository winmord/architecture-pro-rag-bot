Torvin Oak-Scar is a Foreign Wizard, born in 1176 AD during the medieval era. He underwent rigorous training at Hold Grimstone, alongside Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, but his training was cut short due to the Cinderfall Tragedy involving his child, Mira of the Broken Stave. Torvin Oak-Scar later set out as a Foreign Wizard, taking on contracts to hunt monsters and developed a reputation for efficiency and care in his hunts.

Torvin Oak-Scar participated in notable events such as defending Hold Grimstone against The Phantom Cavalcade and fighting alongside Aldric during the Battle of Hold Grimstone. His personality is calm, patient, and stable, with a tendency to avoid eye contact when disagreeing. He has a close bond with Aldric, but his relationship with Morwen of the Obsidian Falls is strained.

Torvin Oak-Scar's physical appearance features a long scar from the corner of his mouth to his ear, as well as hands imbued with powerful magic. The name "Torvin Oak-Scar" originates from Old Norse and means "god cauldron".
