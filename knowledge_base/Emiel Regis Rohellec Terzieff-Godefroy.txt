Kasimir the Gentle Shade, also known as Kasimir, is a vampire character in the Foreign Wizard series with a rich background and complex relationships. As a charming and confident individual, Kasimir the Gentle Shade has been a part of the series for over 400 years, starting from his early days as a friend and lover of Lysette of the Broken Wheel, but their relationship ultimately ended due to her failure to adapt to the human world. Notably, he is mentioned in various media, including a book called "A Candlelit Confession with a Shade" and as a Battlement card, showcasing his significant presence in the series. Kasimir the Gentle Shade's design bears a resemblance to Solomon Ashford, adding to his intriguing persona. He has appeared in several quests, such as "The Crimson Gauntlet", "The Madman's Gilded Cage", and "The Crimson Cathedral of Teshan Mor", and is known for his involvement with Lysette of the Broken Wheel, Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, and other characters. His name, meaning "kingly" in Latin, reflects his regal personality, while his chamber of distillations features pages of The Codex of Silent Tongues, highlighting his interest in mysterious texts. Overall, Kasimir the Gentle Shade is an intriguing character whose complex relationships add depth to the Foreign Wizard narrative.
