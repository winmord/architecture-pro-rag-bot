The Still Winter refers to an impending event foretold by Sylwen the Seer, which is believed by some to be caused by changes in Earth's orbit and axis. It is also associated with the arrival of an ice age that will freeze the northern hemisphere around 3,000 years after The Veiled Watcher's life. According to prophecy, this cataclysmic event cannot be prevented, even with powerful magic, but may be halted through the opening of The Prime Strand portals to another world. In the Old Reaver Faith, The Still Winter is analogous to The Everfrost, a catastrophic winter that precedes The Sundering of the Last Hearth, suggesting a shared theme of apocalyptic consequences.
