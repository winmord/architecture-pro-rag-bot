The Needle of Aether, a fortified peak within Cinderhall Keep on The Veridian Isle, was constructed in the 1240s by The Root-Shrouded Folk using magic to lift heavy stone. The tower featured a portal connecting it to The Spire of the Swallow's Shadow and gained notoriety among Whisperers due to its unpredictable effects when utilized. Following the Ashen Conclave's discovery of the portal, they attempted to block it to prevent further chaos. On July 1st, 1267, a coup led by Elara of the Shifting Sands's uncontrolled use of the portal resulted in a magical explosion that destroyed the tower and ejected Elara into The Ashen Maw.
