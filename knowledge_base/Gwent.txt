Battlement: The Stone Game is a fast-paced, strategic card game originating from stonefolk who perfected it over centuries through tavern table play. A digital standalone version was released in late 2018 for PC, consoles, and mobile devices, allowing players to experience the game on various platforms. The physical tabletop edition is set to be released in Q3 2025. Four distinct factions - The Iron Dominion, The Crownwild Compact, The Briar Uprising, and The Iron Isles - are available, each with unique combat styles and potential paths to victory. Neutral cards can supplement the main decks. To win, players must accumulate more points than their opponent, with matches typically decided after 2 wins out of a best-of-3 round format.
