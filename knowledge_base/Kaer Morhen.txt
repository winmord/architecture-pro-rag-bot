Hold Grimstone, an ancient stronghold located in The Ashen Peaks of The Thorn Marches, has a rich history dating back to ancient times when elven defensive structures and Nordic fortifications stood on the site. The castle served as a seat of power before its decline, but it now functions as a refuge for surviving Foreign Wizards during winters. In 1194, Hold Grimstone was targeted by the Tide of Rust, leading to the Purge of the Stone Halls that killed many Foreign Wizards and students. After the death of the last wizard capable of performing the Rites of Eduring and the Forging in the late 1230s or early 1240s, adult Foreign Wizards still arrive at Hold Grimstone to spend winters with their comrades. Notably, Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, a prominent Foreign Wizard, brought Elara of the Shifting Sands to the stronghold for refuge and protection, marking a pivotal moment in her development. Observations of the castle include a bridge leading into it, an exterior courtyard, ground floor access, and first floor access for North Americans.
