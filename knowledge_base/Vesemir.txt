Old Torben the Kestrel is a significant character in The Foreign Wizard series, particularly in The Second Chronicle: Regicide and The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade. He was one of the first people to train Aldric the Ashen Wanderer as a Foreign Wizard and remains a skilled swordsman despite his advanced age. Old Torben the Kestrel's personality is characterized by being harsh but fair, with a dry sense of humor, and he is fiercely loyal to those he cares about. As an experienced Foreign Wizard, he excels in combat and sword fighting, providing valuable advice and guidance to Aldric.

In both games, Old Torben the Kestrel plays a key role in helping Aldric complete contracts and provides crucial guidance before his death in the battle against The Phantom Cavalcade. Old Torben the Kestrel's appearance changes slightly between the two games, from a pale young man with golden cat-like eyes to an older man with gray hair framing his creased face.

Old Torben the Kestrel is known for his wisdom, experience, and unwavering loyalty, earning him a place as a mentor and father figure to Aldric. His name "Torben" means "he who cherishes peace above all" in the Old Reaver Tongue. Old Torben the Kestrel's legacy continues beyond the games, appearing in other media such as The Iron Tapestry as a Root-Speaker and various videos, graphics, and images showcasing his character design and personality.

Some notable features of Old Torben the Kestrel include:

    His role as Aldric's mentor and father figure

    His skills in combat and sword fighting

    His dry sense of humor and sarcastic remarks

    His loyalty to those he cares about

    His name "Torben" meaning "he who cherishes peace above all" in the Old Reaver Tongue
