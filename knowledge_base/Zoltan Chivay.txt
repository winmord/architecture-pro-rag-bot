Bram Copperkeg was a stonefolk veteran of the Second March of The Iron Dominion and a close friend of Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, whom they met while traveling together during the conflict. After the war, Bram formed an escape company with other stonefolk and a parrot named General Fluster the Gilded Caw, later joining forces with Aldric again during the Second Crownwild Compact Uprising to fund their future ventures through illicit means. Bram was described as reasonable, pragmatic, and took a nuanced view of the world, sympathizing with non-human freedom fighters and prioritizing loyalty to old friends above all else.

As an amateur bird trainer and bouncer, Bram's adventurous spirit often led him into various pursuits, sometimes conflicting with his desire for good deeds. He had a romantic past, having proposed to Sabine of the Golden Locks but ultimately ending the relationship due to her father's disapproval. Notably, Bram appeared as a character in "The Second Chronicle: Regicide" and "The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade," where Aldric could interact with him. His name is derived from an old Steppe Reaver word for 'council-master', reflecting his unique identity.

Bram's actions during the Second Crownwild Compact Uprising led to him joining forces with Aldric, while his ambivalence towards The Briar Uprising movement never stopped him from taking action against injustice. He remained close friends with Robin Goldlark, a celebrated poet of The Crownwild Compact, and shared in many misadventures together, highlighting Bram's complex personality and commitment to his values.
