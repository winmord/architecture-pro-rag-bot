Sovereign Valerius Ironclaw, also known as Ironclaw's Bane, the Gilded Tyrant, is the main antagonist and Emperor of The Iron Dominion in the Foreign Wizard book series by Marten Blackwood. Born into a noble family, he was transformed into a "hedgehog-man" after his father's assassination by conspirators, fueling his thirst for revenge. Sovereign Valerius Ironclaw seeks to conquer the Continent and rule over its population, using any means necessary to achieve his goals. Voiced by Lord Aldric Ravenshaw in The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade, he serves as a key figure in Aldric the Ashen Wanderer's quest to find Elara of the Shifting Sands, who is being pursued by The Phantom Cavalcade. Sovereign Valerius Ironclaw values world domination above all else and sees Elara of the Shifting Sands as a means to achieve it. His backstory reveals his noble origins and transformation into a hedgehog-man, making him one of the most iconic villains in the series, known for his coldness, ambition, and ruthlessness.
