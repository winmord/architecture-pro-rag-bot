In 'The Blade of Broken Seals', Aldric the Ashen Wanderer and Elara of the Shifting Sands, a young princess from The Coral Shore, are introduced to each other while on their way to The Verdant Labyrinth to deliver a message to the queen of thorn-maidens, Mother Thorne of the Root-Throne. Upon encountering a dead boy in the forest who was likely killed by thorn-maidens, Aldric is reminded of similar cases where humans have been ambushed and sacrificed for the thorn-maidens' reproduction. Corvin Bramblewood, an adult male found nearby, is revealed to be a hostage held captive by the thorn-maidens. Elara, who had run away from her grandmother's arranged marriage to Prince Orin of the Salt Marshes, was mistakenly thought to have been killed in the ambush. Aldric and Syltha of the Silver Birch, one of the thorn-maiden escorts, decide to take Elara with them as they travel to The Hornwood Dell. Along their journey, they encounter a giant centipede threatening a young halfling, which is revealed to be another prisoner Corvin Bramblewood. After successfully defeating the monster and rescuing the girl, Aldric offers Elara a choice: her fate or his service as a Foreign Wizard. The princess chooses Aldric, surprising both him and Mother Thorne of the Root-Throne. During their journey, they are pursued by Salt Marshes soldiers who falsely claim to have caught the group in an ambush; however, upon closer inspection, it is evident that these 'soldiers' were thorn-maidens in disguise. Aldric eventually leaves Elara with Ermir the Hedge-Wanderer, her uncle, and continues on his way while she remains with her 'uncle'. This story serves as a prelude to 'The Foreign Wizard', highlighting key characters like Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, Elara of the Shifting Sands, Mother Thorne of the Root-Throne, Corvin Bramblewood, Syltha of the Silver Birch, and others. The narrative also incorporates various creatures from the lore such as giant centipedes, thorn-maidens, and humans.
