Ignis the Golden Coil was a rare golden dragon from the Foreign Wizard lore, known for his skillful polymorphism and cunning tricks, often adopting forms like Corvin of the Ashen Feathers. He first crossed paths with Aldric the Ashen Wanderer during a contract in The Sun-Scorched Steppe, where he used deception to protect two women, Sylvi of the Copper Veil and Yrsa of the Iron Cord, from thieving villagers. Later, while the dragon hunt was on, they joined the group led by Prince Radomir of the Stone March, seeking to slay the large golden dragon sighted near The Scorched Hollow. During this hunt, Ignis the Golden Coil engaged in a fair duel with Ser Aldric of the Ivory Shield and defeated him, revealing his true form as a dragon. Despite being outnumbered by a militia led by Grimm the Wool-Monger, the dragon protected a newborn hatchling, which ignited anger among the group leaders who wanted to kill it. Ignis the Golden Coil's actions and motives were complex; he was fiercely protective of his kin and their offspring, but also cautious about wielding weapons due to a past experience with Aldric. His interactions with Aldric and Morwen of the Obsidian Falls illustrate the nuanced relationship between dragons and humans in the Foreign Wizard universe, hinting at the existence of golden dragons as mythical beings contradicting common beliefs. Over time, Ignis the Golden Coil raised his daughter, Emberlyn the Golden Dawn, teaching her valuable life lessons before he vanished into the ether.
