Lady Sevren the Goshawk is a significant character in the Foreign Wizard universe, known for her exceptional magical abilities. She was once an advisor to Sovereign Valdemar the Keen but played a role in his death and was implicated in the murder of other monarchs, including Sovereign Marik of Greywater and Sovereign Aldwyn of Thornwood. Lady Sevren the Goshawk founded The Gilded Circle, an organization that aimed to promote the interests of the Whisperers, despite questionable methods.

Her complicated relationships with characters include Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, Morwen of the Obsidian Falls, Sovereign Radomir the Stern, Mira of the Silver Quill, Grenn Oak-Shoulder, Helga Copper-Voice, Katrin of the Ashen Shawl, Janus the Iron Grip, and Gunnar Stone-Brow. Lady Sevren the Goshawk possesses polymorphing ability, allowing her to transform into an owl, and exceptional magical abilities. Her ultimate goal is to revive The Gilded Circle and secure her place in history as a powerful Whisperer.

Notable quests featuring Lady Sevren the Goshawk include The Owl's Gambit (The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade), The Last Turning of the Wheel (The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade), and The Gilded Throne's Fall (The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade). Historically, Lady Sevren the Goshawk prefers women, as demonstrated by references in The Second Chronicle: Regicide and The Season of Scorn.
