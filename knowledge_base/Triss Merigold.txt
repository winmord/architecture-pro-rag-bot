Rowena of the Copper Grove is a central character in The Foreign Wizard series, serving as the main romance option for Aldric the Ashen Wanderer in both games. A powerful Whisperer from Copperhold, she possesses exceptional magical abilities, which she uses to support Aldric and navigate the complexities of his world. Rowena has a long history with Aldric, having been one of his closest friends since their meeting at The Gilded Chase of Thornwood, and her relationship with him is central to the story.

Rowena's personality is characterized by strength, independence, and a deep commitment to those she cares about, making her a fiercely loyal ally. Her magical abilities are unmatched, allowing her to use intellect and spell-casting prowess to overcome obstacles. Throughout both games, Rowena plays an important role in Aldric's journey, providing emotional support and comfort.

Rowena has a complex backstory, having been a member of the royal council of Sovereign Aldwyn of Thornwood before helping Aldric clear his name and avenge his adopted daughter Elara of the Shifting Sands. Her history with Aldric is intertwined with her involvement in The Gilded Circle, a secret organization of female Whisperers that she considers one of its most talented members.

As a character, Rowena's appearance has been depicted slightly differently between books and games, but her personality and magical abilities remain consistent. She is known for her beauty, intelligence, and charm, as well as her willingness to risk her own life to help others. With a bond that runs deeper than a superficial friendship with Aldric, Rowena of the Copper Grove remains a core part of the story throughout The Foreign Wizard series.
