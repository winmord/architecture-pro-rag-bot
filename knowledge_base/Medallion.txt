The Foreign Wizard Iron Ward is a significant artifact within the lore, often referenced across various games and books. In 'The Foreign Wizard' series, it represents a powerful magical item associated with Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, one of its primary characters. Specifically, there are two distinct types: Sovereign Aldwyn's Iron Ward from 'The Second Chronicle: Regicide,' and Lady Sevren's Iron Ward from the same game. Additionally, Torben the Elder's Iron Ward, a quest item in 'The Foreign Wizard' video game series, is also referred to as the Iron Ward. Each of these Iron Wards carries unique magical properties and are integral to specific storylines and quests within their respective games.
