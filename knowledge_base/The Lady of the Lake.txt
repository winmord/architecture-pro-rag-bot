The seventh book in The Foreign Wizard series, "The Veiled Mother of the Mere," by Marten Blackwood, follows Elara of the Shifting Sands as she navigates a complex web of time and world. Trapped in an Elven world, she must escape to reunite with Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, her friends, and confront her worst fears. Sylwen of the Dreaming Veil's study of Aldric's legend through dreams and images serves as a key aspect of the narrative, which shifts between different timelines featuring various characters. The story culminates at The Mercy at Stonebridge, where The Crownwild Compact defeats The Iron Dominion army. Elara later saves Morwen of the Obsidian Falls from Lord Malachar the Broken Star's castle, leading to a reunion with Aldric and his companions before they part ways to explore new paths. Key events in the book are intertwined with historical contexts, including the war-torn world outside the Elven realm, and Corvin Black-Crow's pursuit of Elara. Ultimately, the story concludes with Elara reuniting with her group, marking a turning point in her journey.
