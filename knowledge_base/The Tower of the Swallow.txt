In 'The Spire of Falling Stars' by Marten Blackwood, a girl named Elara of the Shifting Sands's life story unfolds through Old Bromir of the Sinking Mire, an elderly swamp dweller who nurses her back to health. She reveals how Corvin Black-Crow, after killing all bandits except for Elara, captured and imprisoned her. Meanwhile, Aldric the Ashen Wanderer is on a quest to find Root-Speakers who may know of Elara's whereabouts, with Elandor the Silver-Tongued guiding him about the prophecy involving her. Morwen of the Obsidian Falls attempts to locate Lord Malachar the Broken Star, adding complexity to Aldric's search for Elara. The book's translations vary across regions, with 'Falling Stars' appearing in both singular and plural forms. This title reflects a single entity, though it's unclear if this is a linguistic or editorial choice.
