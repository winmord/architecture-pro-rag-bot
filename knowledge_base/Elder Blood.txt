The concept of The Prime Strand originates from the Shrouded Weavers who created a genetic program to produce an exceptionally gifted child. This "Heir of the Prime Strand" was expected to save the elves from annihilation, but its last carrier, Lira of the Silver Bough, unexpectedly bonded with a human instead of an elf, disrupting the prophecy. As a result, descendants of those who possess the blood can inherit it and include notable carriers such as Corvin Stonehand, Elara of the Shifting Sands, Kaspar of the Iron Dial, Rosalind of the Foaming Wave, Brynhild of the Raven Crest, Isolde of the Thorn Grove, Velimir the Silent, Sabine Shallowford, Dermot Coal-eye, Stellan Grim-brow, Mara of the Cinder Crown, Yvaine of the Pale Tarn, and others with the "kindled mark" or "dormant thread". The blood's nature is debated, with some viewing it as a rare inherited talent granting control over time and space, while others see it as a curse passed down through generations, carrying death and destruction within its power.
