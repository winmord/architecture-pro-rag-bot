The 'Ashford Purge' is a tragic event described in 'The Foreign Wizard' books, recounted by anonymous authors based on eyewitness accounts. Following peace talks with The Iron Dominion, the people of Ashford suffered poverty and resentment due to lackluster reparations from kings and magnates. The non-human population, including flesh-mimics, stonefolk, the Shrouded Folk, and Whisperers like Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, faced persecution. A riotous mob targeted anyone deemed different or accused of using spells, resulting in the deaths of many and unrest that spilled over into looting, rape, and setting scores. Notably, stonefolk Cairn of the Copper Cask and Shrouded Folk Rennic the Ash-Brow were among those slain. Aldric the Ashen Wanderer attempted to protect his companions but was struck with a pitchfork and killed. Rowena of the Copper Grove intervened with a powerful hailstorm spell, saving many lives. Despite these efforts, the exact fate of Aldric's body remains unknown.
