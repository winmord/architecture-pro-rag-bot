Elara of the Shifting Sands, also known as Elara of the Shifting Sands, is the main protagonist with a complex and troubled past. She is on the run from The Phantom Cavalcade, led by Sovereign Valerius Ironclaw, who seeks to capture her for his own purposes. Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, a Foreign Wizard and Elara's adoptive father, joins her on her journey and helps navigate the dangers they face. As a Prime Strand, Elara struggles with her powers and grapples with her identity, royal heritage, and relationships with Aldric and other characters. Her destiny is deeply connected to Aldric's, and their relationship is complex due to the Serpent's Knot. The game's storyline unfolds through a series of escalating battles and confrontations with The Phantom Cavalcade, as well as numerous side quests and character interactions that deepen Elara's backstory and motivations.

Notable themes include the struggle for power and control among factions vying for Elara and her Prime Strand powers, as well as complex relationships between Elara, Aldric, and other characters. Elara's journey is marked by challenges such as a quarrel with witch-women in The Blighted Moor, a confrontation with powerful beings known as The Weeping Sisters, and discovering facts about her lineage that shake her to her core. Throughout the game, she faces numerous trials, including a visit to Aldric with no accompanying reward, facing The Gilded Circle and its powerful sorceresses, and ultimately must face her destiny to stop The Still Winter.
