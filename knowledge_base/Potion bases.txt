In The Foreign Wizard lore, potions are created using standard, high, or top quality Tincture roots and vitriol salts, which range from throat-burning pepper vodka to exotic elixirs. Standard quality allows up to three ingredients per potion, while high and top quality enable four and five respectively. Each category has distinct varieties such as Iron Dominion Sour (high), Northmarch Verdant (top), March-warden's Gut-Warm (standard), Thornwood Amber (high), Bloodstone Draught (top), Stonefolk Ember (top), Shadow-berry Liqueur (top), Thornwood White (top), Sun-Kissed Fire (top), Aether-Heart Essence (top), Quicksilver Bane (top), Root-Weaver's Nectar, Bitter Warden (all top quality), and Scale-Wort Venom (top quality). The Pale Shearwater is the highest quality Tincture roots and vitriol salts, combining any lower quality base with additional alchemical components.
