Aldric the Ashen Wanderer trained as a Foreign Wizard under Old Torben the Kestrel at the Order of the Iron Fangs. Rorik the Bitter Hound, another Foreign Wizard trained by Old Torben the Kestrel, was known for his rough demeanor and clashed frequently with Aldric due to their differing views on being a Foreign Wizard. Rorik's past involved physical abuse by his alcoholic father, which led him to seek protection through the Debt of the Unlooked-For that made him a Foreign Wizard. He completed trials like the Ordeal of the Iron Ward to prove himself worthy. Rorik later trained with Torvin Oak-Scar and Old Torben the Kestrel before helping Rowena of the Copper Grove during her illness, participating in the Battle of Hold Grimstone, and potentially becoming romantically involved with Sabine of the Amber Veil depending on player choices.
