The 'Weave-Collapse' is a historically significant book from The Gilded Quill Studio's Foreign Wizard games, detailing a cosmic event that occurred 1500 years ago during which numerous creatures from parallel universes were accidentally introduced into our reality. These include carrion-crawlers and grave-wraiths, often referred to as relics of the Weave-Collapse by the Shrouded Folk who claim humans arrived in this world around the same time they mastered magic, contradicting their own belief that humans are newcomers from the Weave-Collapse. The book is found on numerous bookshelves across all three Foreign Wizard games and can be purchased or randomly looted. In 'The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade', it's available as random loot in various houses around The Sun-Drenched Vale, while in 'Phantom Cavalcade' expansion Crimson Vintage, it can be found in Palebrook's warehouse by the town hall. Despite its complex title, the Weave-Collapse is explained in simple terms: when two distant vessels (worlds) collide on a cosmic sea, some crew members from one ship may move to another, and cargo can spill onto neighboring ships. This event led to the arrival of monsters like carrion-crawlers and stone-gazers, while humans learned the arcane mysteries of magic unbeknownst to them beforehand. The book also hints at scholars' tendency to describe simple phenomena in overly complex terms.
