The Iron Runes, a book containing simple spells known as 'Runes', is randomly lootable but consistently identified by the game's randomization bug as one specific book among approximately 15 scattered throughout The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade. This book can be found in Rowena of the Copper Grove's ransacked house on The Hierophant's Circle, a skeleton near gargoyles during 'The Trail of Broken Blades', and a bookshelf in the room with a key to the library during 'The Spire of Sudden Shadows'. Despite being able to cast simple spells like The Push of Emptiness (telekinesis), The Breath of Embers (pyrokinesis), and The Whisper of Still Minds (hypnosis), Foreign Wizards' casting of real magic is met with distrust, stemming from their lack of inborn magical talents gained during The Rite of Thorns. Their fear of teleportation, a dangerous practice, likely stems from the rare occurrence of deadly accidents during translocational travel.
