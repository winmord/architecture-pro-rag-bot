Morwen of the Obsidian Falls is a complex and significant character in the Foreign Wizard series, known for her exceptional magical abilities and tumultuous personality. Born into a pathological family with mixed human-elven bloodline, Morwen was emotionally abused by her father and rejected by her mother, leading to an attempt at suicide as a student at The Spire of Tides's first class. Despite this traumatic start, she excelled in her studies under the guidance of Grandweaver Seraphine of the Silver Thread and became a highly talented Whisperer after undergoing magical corrections for her deformities.

Morwen's relationship with Aldric the Ashen Wanderer is complex and central to the story, spanning across multiple games and media appearances. In The Second Chronicle: Regicide, she becomes involved in Aldric's quest against The Iron Dominion and provides crucial magical assistance, while in The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade, her storyline intertwines deeply with Aldric's as they search for Elara of the Shifting Sands, his adopted daughter. Morwen is also a key character in several comic book series, including The Raven's Due and The Slayer's Oath.

Physically, Morwen has curly black hair, pale skin, and piercing violet eyes, and is described as having a slender build. Her beauty is enhanced by her magical abilities, making her one of the most striking characters in the Foreign Wizard series. With her complex personality reflecting her tumultuous past, Morwen is intelligent, determined, and resourceful, often helping Aldric on his adventures.

Morwen's character has been portrayed by actress Lady Mira Stonewood in The Iron Tapestry (2019-present).
