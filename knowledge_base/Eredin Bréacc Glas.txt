Maeloch the Winter Shard, also known as the Chieftain of the Phantom Cavalcade, is a primary antagonist in The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade. He is a ruthless warrior and leader of the elite cavalry unit known as The Crimson Reapers, who hunt humans to bring them back to his world. Maeloch the Winter Shard's past involves serving under Sovereign Oriniel Everdew before poisoning him to take the throne, gaining power over The Thresholds of the Sundered Sky, which allow him to travel between dimensions and invade other worlds. His ultimate goal is to open gates between worlds, bringing about a catastrophic event that will allow The Shrouded Folk to escape their doomed world and conquer the Continent. Maeloch the Winter Shard possesses exceptional combat skills, can control undead forces, and has no qualms about killing or enslaving humans. He is cunning, power-hungry, and has connections to key characters in the game, including Aldric the Ashen Wanderer, Elara of the Shifting Sands, Morwen of the Obsidian Falls, and Robin Goldlark, making him a formidable opponent for Aldric in their epic confrontation.
