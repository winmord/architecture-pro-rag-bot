Raven of the Dust Road was an intelligence officer who served in The Iron Dominion. He was born in Vicovaro and became involved in Aldric the Ashen Wanderer's quest to find Elara of the Shifting Sands. After being imprisoned for failing to capture her, he joined Aldric's company after helping them escape. Despite being instructed by his emperor to bring Elara back, Raven of the Dust Road continued to secretly follow Aldric's group as they journeyed across the Continent. He eventually formed an alliance with them to rescue Elara from Sovereign Valerius Ironclaw's forces, joining forces at some point in their quest.
