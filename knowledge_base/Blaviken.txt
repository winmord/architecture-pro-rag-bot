Dunmire is a coastal town in The Greydeep, historically part of King Torvald the Stony's realm and later annexed by Northmarch during the Reaping Years. Known for its notable residents including Aldwyn the Elder, Master Krethor, Helvet the Shrouded, Mira of the Salt Winds, Petra Cobblefield, Grim Stonefoot, Fendrel the Scarred, Lisek the Sparrow, and Old Yaromir, as well as his shade-hound friend Old Yaromir's mire-kin. The town is home to various locations such as Krethor's Needle, The Gilded Yard, The Silver Herring, The Hollow Circle, and Greyfen Hall. In The Foreign Wizard's Chronicle, references to Dunmire and its inhabitants are prominently featured, referencing Aldric the Ashen Wanderer's encounters with the town during his travels.
