In the Foreign Wizard universe, magic, or 'the Whisperings', is a multifaceted and powerful force that can be harnessed by various practitioners including Foreign Wizards, the Whisperers, the Root-Speakers, the Bell-Keepers, the Knowing Folk, and Rowena of the Copper Grove. Magic stems from nature and encompasses elements such as fire, water, earth, air, mind, and divination. It is viewed as both an elite discipline requiring artistry and talent (Art), a dangerous force capable of destruction when wielded recklessly (Chaos), and a scientific process that brings development and answers to mankind (Science). Magic can be manipulated through physical means ('-kinesis'), communication ('mancy'), or innate abilities, with each branch having its own specific skills and banned practices. Foreign Wizards like Aldric the Ashen Wanderer utilize simple signs for healing, teleportation, destruction, illusion creation, and form alteration, while the Whisperers possess a more extensive knowledge of magical arts. The power source for spellcasting is the elements, with fire being the most potent yet risky due to the vulnerability to water. Notably, some magic users, like Shadow-Summoners or Bone-Whisperers, are taboo and have been outlawed by certain Whisperer organizations.
