Eredin Bréacc Glas, also known as the King of the Wild Hunt, is a primary antagonist in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt. He is a ruthless warrior and leader of the elite cavalry unit known as the Red Riders, who hunt humans to bring them back to his world. Eredin's past involves serving under King Auberon Muircetach before poisoning him to take the throne, gaining power over the Gates of the Worlds, which allow him to travel between dimensions and invade other worlds. His ultimate goal is to open gates between worlds, bringing about a catastrophic event that will allow the Aen Elle to escape their doomed world and conquer the Continent. Eredin possesses exceptional combat skills, can control undead forces, and has no qualms about killing or enslaving humans. He is cunning, power-hungry, and has connections to key characters in the game, including Geralt of Rivia, Ciri, Yennefer, and Dandelion, making him a formidable opponent for Geralt in their epic confrontation.
