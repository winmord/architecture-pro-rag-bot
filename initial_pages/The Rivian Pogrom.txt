The 'Rivian Pogrom' is a tragic event described in 'The Witcher' books, recounted by anonymous authors based on eyewitness accounts. Following peace talks with Nilfgaard, the people of Rivia suffered poverty and resentment due to lackluster reparations from kings and magnates. The non-human population, including changelings, dwarves, elves, and sorcerers like Geralt of Rivia, faced persecution. A riotous mob targeted anyone deemed different or accused of using spells, resulting in the deaths of many and unrest that spilled over into looting, rape, and setting scores. Notably, dwarf Thane Cahir and elf Gaspard were among those slain. Geralt of Rivia attempted to protect his companions but was struck with a pitchfork and killed. Triss Merigold intervened with a powerful hailstorm spell, saving many lives. Despite these efforts, the exact fate of Geralt's body remains unknown.
