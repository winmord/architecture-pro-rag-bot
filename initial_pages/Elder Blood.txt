The concept of Elder Blood originates from elven mages who created a genetic program to produce an exceptionally gifted child. This "Child of Elder Blood" was expected to save the elves from annihilation, but its last carrier, Lara Dorren, unexpectedly bonded with a human instead of an elf, disrupting the prophecy. As a result, descendants of those who possess the blood can inherit it and include notable carriers such as Alvin, Ciri, Jacques de Aldersberg, Pavetta, Riannon, Adalia, Amavet, Muriel, Corbett, Dagorad, Falka, Fiona, and others with the "activator" or "latent" gene. The blood's nature is debated, with some viewing it as a rare inherited talent granting control over time and space, while others see it as a curse passed down through generations, carrying death and destruction within its power.
