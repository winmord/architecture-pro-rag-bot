Villentretenmerth was a rare golden dragon from the Witcher lore, known for his skillful polymorphism and cunning tricks, often adopting forms like Borch Three Jackdaws. He first crossed paths with Geralt during a contract in Zerrikin, where he used deception to protect two women, Téa and Véa, from thieving villagers. Later, while the dragon hunt was on, they joined the group led by Prince Niedamir, seeking to slay the large golden dragon sighted near Barefield. During this hunt, Villentretenmerth engaged in a fair duel with Sir Eyck and defeated him, revealing his true form as a dragon. Despite being outnumbered by a militia led by Sheepbagger, the dragon protected a newborn hatchling, which ignited anger among the group leaders who wanted to kill it. Villentretenmerth's actions and motives were complex; he was fiercely protective of his kin and their offspring, but also cautious about wielding weapons due to a past experience with Geralt. His interactions with Geralt and Yennefer illustrate the nuanced relationship between dragons and humans in the Witcher universe, hinting at the existence of golden dragons as mythical beings contradicting common beliefs. Over time, Villentretenmerth raised his daughter, Saesenthessis, teaching her valuable life lessons before he vanished into the ether.
