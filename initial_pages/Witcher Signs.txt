Witcher Signs, a book containing simple spells known as 'Signs', is randomly lootable but consistently identified by the game's randomization bug as one specific book among approximately 15 scattered throughout The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt. This book can be found in Triss' ransacked house on Hierarch Square, a skeleton near gargoyles during 'The Path of Warriors', and a bookshelf in the room with a key to the library during 'The Tower Outta Nowheres'. Despite being able to cast simple spells like Aard (telekinesis), Igni (pyrokinesis), and Axii (hypnosis), witchers' casting of real magic is met with distrust, stemming from their lack of inborn magical talents gained during the Trial of the Grasses. Their fear of teleportation, a dangerous practice, likely stems from the rare occurrence of deadly accidents during translocational travel.
