Emhyr var Emreis, also known as Deithwen Addan yn Carn aep Morvudd, is the main antagonist and Emperor of Nilfgaard in the Witcher book series by Andrzej Sapkowski. Born into a noble family, he was transformed into a "hedgehog-man" after his father's assassination by conspirators, fueling his thirst for revenge. Emhyr seeks to conquer the Continent and rule over its population, using any means necessary to achieve his goals. Voiced by Charles Dance in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, he serves as a key figure in Geralt of Rivia's quest to find Ciri, who is being pursued by the Wild Hunt. Emhyr values world domination above all else and sees Ciri as a means to achieve it. His backstory reveals his noble origins and transformation into a hedgehog-man, making him one of the most iconic villains in the series, known for his coldness, ambition, and ruthlessness.
