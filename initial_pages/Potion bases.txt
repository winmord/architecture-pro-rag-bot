In The Witcher lore, potions are created using standard, high, or top quality potion bases, which range from throat-burning pepper vodka to exotic elixirs. Standard quality allows up to three ingredients per potion, while high and top quality enable four and five respectively. Each category has distinct varieties such as Nilfgaardian Lemon (high), Redanian Herbal (top), Soldier's Hooch (standard), Temerian Rye (high), Cherry Spirit Cordial (top), Dwarven Spirit (top), Plum Cordial (top), Temerian spirit (top), Zerrikanian spirit (top), Alcohest (top), Azoth (top), Mandrake cordial, Wormwood spirit (all top quality), and Wyvern blood spirit (top quality). White Gull is the highest quality potion base, combining any lower quality base with additional alchemical components.
