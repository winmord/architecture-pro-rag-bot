Geralt of Rivia trained as a witcher under Vesemir at the School of the Wolf. Lambert, another witcher trained by Vesemir, was known for his rough demeanor and clashed frequently with Geralt due to their differing views on being a witcher. Lambert's past involved physical abuse by his alcoholic father, which led him to seek protection through the Law of Surprise that made him a witcher. He completed trials like the Trial of the Medallion to prove himself worthy. Lambert later trained with Eskel and Vesemir before helping Triss during her illness, participating in the Battle of Kaer Morhen, and potentially becoming romantically involved with Keira Metz depending on player choices.
