Cahir Mawr Dyffryn aep Ceallach was an intelligence officer who served in the Nilfgaardian Empire. He was born in Vicovaro and became involved in Geralt of Rivia's quest to find Ciri. After being imprisoned for failing to capture her, he joined Geralt's company after helping them escape. Despite being instructed by his emperor to bring Ciri back, Cahir continued to secretly follow Geralt's group as they journeyed across the Continent. He eventually formed an alliance with them to rescue Ciri from Emperor Emhyr var Emreis' forces, joining forces at some point in their quest.
