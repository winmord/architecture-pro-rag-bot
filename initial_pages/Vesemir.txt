Vesemir is a significant character in The Witcher series, particularly in The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings and The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt. He was one of the first people to train Geralt of Rivia as a witcher and remains a skilled swordsman despite his advanced age. Vesemir's personality is characterized by being harsh but fair, with a dry sense of humor, and he is fiercely loyal to those he cares about. As an experienced witcher, he excels in combat and sword fighting, providing valuable advice and guidance to Geralt.

In both games, Vesemir plays a key role in helping Geralt complete contracts and provides crucial guidance before his death in the battle against the Wild Hunt. Vesemir's appearance changes slightly between the two games, from a pale young man with golden cat-like eyes to an older man with gray hair framing his creased face.

Vesemir is known for his wisdom, experience, and unwavering loyalty, earning him a place as a mentor and father figure to Geralt. His name "Wyszomir" means "he who cherishes peace above all" in Polish. Vesemir's legacy continues beyond the games, appearing in other media such as The Hexer TV series as a druid and various videos, graphics, and images showcasing his character design and personality.

Some notable features of Vesemir include:
- His role as Geralt's mentor and father figure
- His skills in combat and sword fighting
- His dry sense of humor and sarcastic remarks
- His loyalty to those he cares about
- His name "Wyszomir" meaning "he who cherishes peace above all" in Polish
