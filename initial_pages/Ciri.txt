In "The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt", Ciri, also known as Cirilla Fiona Elen Riannon, is the main protagonist with a complex and troubled past. She is on the run from the Wild Hunt, led by Emhyr var Emreis, who seeks to capture her for his own purposes. Geralt of Rivia, a Witcher and Ciri's adoptive father, joins her on her journey and helps navigate the dangers they face. As an Elder Blood, Ciri struggles with her powers and grapples with her identity, royal heritage, and relationships with Geralt and other characters. Her destiny is deeply connected to Geralt's, and their relationship is complex due to Fate's tangled fetters. The game's storyline unfolds through a series of escalating battles and confrontations with the Wild Hunt, as well as numerous side quests and character interactions that deepen Ciri's backstory and motivations.

Notable themes include the struggle for power and control among factions vying for Ciri and her Elder Blood powers, as well as complex relationships between Ciri, Geralt, and other characters. Ciri's journey is marked by challenges such as a quarrel with witch-women in Velen, a confrontation with powerful beings known as the Crones, and discovering facts about her lineage that shake her to her core. Throughout the game, she faces numerous trials, including a visit to Geralt with no accompanying reward, facing the Lodge and its powerful sorceresses, and ultimately must face her destiny to stop the White Frost.

Ciri's appearance has been depicted in various media, including concept art, paintings, the official cosplay guide, The Hexer TV series, Netflix's The Witcher, and the 2024 reveal trailer for The Witcher IV.
