Eskel is a witcher, born in 1176 AD during the medieval era. He underwent rigorous training at Kaer Morhen, alongside Geralt of Rivia, but his training was cut short due to an accident involving his child, Deidre Ademeyn. Eskel later set out as a witcher, taking on contracts to hunt monsters and developed a reputation for efficiency and care in his hunts.

Eskel participated in notable events such as defending Kaer Morhen against the Wild Hunt and fighting alongside Geralt during the Battle of Kaer Morhen. His personality is calm, patient, and stable, with a tendency to avoid eye contact when disagreeing. He has a close bond with Geralt, but his relationship with Yennefer is strained.

Eskel's physical appearance features a long scar from the corner of his mouth to his ear, as well as hands imbued with powerful magic that can cause pleasant vibrations in mages. The name "Eskel" originates from Old Norse and means "god cauldron".
