Zoltan Chivay was a dwarf veteran of the Second Nilfgaard War and a close friend of Geralt of Rivia, whom they met while traveling together during the conflict. After the war, Zoltan formed an escape company with other dwarves and a parrot named Field Marshal Windbag, later joining forces with Geralt again during the Second Northern War to fund their future ventures through illicit means. Zoltan was described as reasonable, pragmatic, and took a nuanced view of the world, sympathizing with non-human freedom fighters and prioritizing loyalty to old friends above all else.

As an amateur bird trainer and bouncer, Zoltan's adventurous spirit often led him into various pursuits, sometimes conflicting with his desire for good deeds. He had a romantic past, having proposed to Eudora but ultimately ending the relationship due to her father's disapproval. Notably, Zoltan appeared as a character in "The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings" and "The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt," where Geralt could interact with him. His name is derived from an old Hungarian word for 'sultan', reflecting his unique identity.

Zoltan's actions during the Second Northern War led to him joining forces with Geralt, while his ambivalence towards the Scoia'tael movement never stopped him from taking action against injustice. He remained close friends with Dandelion, a celebrated poet of the North, and shared in many misadventures together, highlighting Zoltan's complex personality and commitment to his values.
