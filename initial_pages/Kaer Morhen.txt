Kaer Morhen, an ancient stronghold located in the Blue Mountains of Hertch, has a rich history dating back to ancient times when elven defensive structures and Nordic fortifications stood on the site. The castle served as a seat of power before its decline, but it now functions as a refuge for surviving witchers during winters. In 1194, Kaer Morhen was targeted by anti-witcher sentiment, leading to a pogrom that killed many witchers and students. After the death of the last wizard capable of performing the Trials and Changes in the late 1230s or early 1240s, adult witchers still arrive at Kaer Morhen to spend winters with their comrades. Notably, Geralt of Rivia, a prominent witcher, brought Ciri to the stronghold for refuge and protection, marking a pivotal moment in her development. Observations of the castle include a bridge leading into it, an exterior courtyard, ground floor access, and first floor access for North Americans.
