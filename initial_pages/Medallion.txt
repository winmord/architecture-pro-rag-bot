The Witcher medallion is a significant artifact within the lore, often referenced across various games and books. In 'The Witcher' series, it represents a powerful magical item associated with Geralt of Rivia, one of its primary characters. Specifically, there are two distinct types: Henselt's Medallion from 'The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings,' and Philippa Eilhart's Medallion from the same game. Additionally, Berengar's Medallion, a quest item in 'The Witcher' video game series, is also referred to as the medallion. Each of these medallions carries unique magical properties and are integral to specific storylines and quests within their respective games.
