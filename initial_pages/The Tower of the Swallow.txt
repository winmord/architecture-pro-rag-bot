In 'The Tower of the Swallow' by Andrzej Sapkowski, a girl named Ciri's life story unfolds through Vysogota, an elderly swamp dweller who nurses her back to health. She reveals how Leo Bonhart, after killing all bandits except for Ciri, captured and imprisoned her. Meanwhile, Geralt is on a quest to find Druids who may know of Ciri's whereabouts, with Avallac'h guiding him about the prophecy involving her. Yennefer attempts to locate Vilgefortz, adding complexity to Geralt's search for Ciri. The book's translations vary across regions, with 'Swallow' appearing in both singular and plural forms. This title reflects a single entity, though it's unclear if this is a linguistic or editorial choice.
