Triss Merigold is a central character in The Witcher series, serving as the main romance option for Geralt of Rivia in both games. A powerful sorceress from Maribor, she possesses exceptional magical abilities, which she uses to support Geralt and navigate the complexities of his world. Triss has a long history with Geralt, having been one of his closest friends since their meeting at the Royal Hunt competition in Vizima, and her relationship with him is central to the story.

Triss's personality is characterized by strength, independence, and a deep commitment to those she cares about, making her a fiercely loyal ally. Her magical abilities are unmatched, allowing her to use intellect and spell-casting prowess to overcome obstacles. Throughout both games, Triss plays an important role in Geralt's journey, providing emotional support and comfort.

Triss has a complex backstory, having been a member of the royal council of King Foltest of Temeria before helping Geralt clear his name and avenge his adopted daughter Ciri. Her history with Geralt is intertwined with her involvement in the Lodge of Sorceresses, a secret organization of female mages that she considers one of its most talented members.

As a character, Triss's appearance has been depicted slightly differently between books and games, but her personality and magical abilities remain consistent. She is known for her beauty, intelligence, and charm, as well as her willingness to risk her own life to help others. With a bond that runs deeper than a superficial friendship with Geralt, Triss Merigold remains a core part of the story throughout The Witcher series.
