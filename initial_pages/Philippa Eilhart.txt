Philippa Eilhart is a significant character in the Witcher universe, known for her exceptional magical abilities. She was once an advisor to King Vizimir II but played a role in his death and was implicated in the murder of other monarchs, including Demavend of Aedirn and Foltest of Temeria. Philippa founded the Lodge of Sorceresses, an organization that aimed to promote the interests of sorcerers, despite questionable methods.

Her complicated relationships with characters include Geralt of Rivia, Yennefer of Vengerberg, Radovid, Cynthia, Skalen Burdon, Marti Södergren, Sabrina Glevissig, Sigismund Dijkstra, and Yarpen Zigrin. Philippa possesses polymorphing ability, allowing her to transform into an owl, and exceptional magical abilities. Her ultimate goal is to revive the Lodge of Sorceresses and secure her place in history as a powerful sorceress.

Notable quests featuring Philippa include Blindingly Obvious (The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt), Final Preparations (The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt), and Reason of State (The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt). Historically, Philippa prefers women, as demonstrated by references in The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings and Time of Contempt.
