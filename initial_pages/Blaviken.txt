Blaviken is a coastal town in Arcsea, historically part of King Audoen's realm and later annexed by Redania during the 'Butchering' period. Known for its notable residents including Caldemeyn, Irion, Stregobor, Libushe, Marilka, Carrypebble, Borg, Karelka, and Dziega, as well as his werewolf friend Dziega's werebbubb. The town is home to various locations such as Irion's tower, the Golden Court, The Tuna Fish, Marketplace, and Huntington Manor. In 'The Witcher' game, references to Blaviken and its inhabitants are prominently featured, referencing Geralt's encounters with the town during his travels.
