In 'The Sword of Destiny', Geralt of Rivia and Ciri, a young princess from Cintra, are introduced to each other while on their way to Brokilon to deliver a message to the queen of dryads, Eithné. Upon encountering a dead boy in the forest who was likely killed by dryads, Geralt is reminded of similar cases where humans have been ambushed and sacrificed for the dryads' reproduction. Freixenet, an adult male found nearby, is revealed to be a hostage held captive by the dryads. Ciri, who had run away from her grandmother's arranged marriage to Prince Kistrin of Verden, was mistakenly thought to have been killed in the ambush. Geralt and Braenn, one of the dryad escorts, decide to take Ciri with them as they travel to Duén Canell. Along their journey, they encounter a giant centipede threatening a young halfling, which is revealed to be another prisoner Freixenet. After successfully defeating the monster and rescuing the girl, Geralt offers Ciri a choice: her fate or his service as a witcher. The princess chooses Geralt, surprising both him and Eithné. During their journey, they are pursued by Verden soldiers who falsely claim to have caught the group in an ambush; however, upon closer inspection, it is evident that these 'soldiers' were dryads in disguise. Geralt eventually leaves Ciri with Mousesack, her uncle, and continues on his way while she remains with her 'uncle'. This story serves as a prelude to 'The Witcher', highlighting key characters like Geralt of Rivia, Ciri, Eithné, Freixenet, Braenn, and others. The narrative also incorporates various creatures from the lore such as giant centipedes, dryads, and humans.
