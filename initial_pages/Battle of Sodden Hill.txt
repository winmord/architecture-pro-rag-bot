The Battle of Sodden Hill, also known as the Second battle of Sodden, was a significant conflict during the First War with Nilfgaard, taking place in Lower Sodden. This engagement involved twenty-two mages from various factions including the Northern Realms, Temerian Army, Kaedwenian Army, Royal Redanian Army, Aedirnian Army, Brotherhood of Sorcerers, Skellige reinforcements, and several other mages. The hill, referred to as 'Mage Hill', 'Kite Hill', or the 'Hill of the Fourteen' due to fourteen mage deaths, is notable for thirteen confirmed casualties and one hundred thousand participants, with at least thirty thousand killed or wounded. Field-marshal Coehoorn's disastrous defeat marked this battle, contrary to previous victories. The exact number of bodies buried on the hill is debated, but it includes Triss Merigold and Yennefer, though only Yoël Grethen from the fourteen was found among them. Each year at Belleteyn, fires are lit to commemorate the fallen mages. The battlefield, as described in Ciri's visions, is depicted as a vast sea of grass overlooking undulating fields covered by lush grass and flat-topped hills with mage stands.
