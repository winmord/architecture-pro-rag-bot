Regis, also known as Godefroy, is a vampire character in the Witcher series with a rich background and complex relationships. As a charming and confident individual, Regis has been a part of the series for over 400 years, starting from his early days as a friend and lover of Syanna, but their relationship ultimately ended due to her failure to adapt to the human world. Notably, he is mentioned in various media, including a book called "My Evening with a Vampire" and as a gwent card, showcasing his significant presence in the series. Regis's design bears a resemblance to Peter Cushing, adding to his intriguing persona. He has appeared in several quests, such as "Blood Run", "La Cage au Fou", and "Tesham Mutna", and is known for his involvement with Syanna, Geralt, and other characters. His name, meaning "kingly" in Latin, reflects his regal personality, while his lab features pages of the Voynich Manuscript, highlighting his interest in mysterious texts. Overall, Regis is an intriguing character whose complex relationships add depth to the Witcher narrative.
