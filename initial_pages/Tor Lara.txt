Tor Lara, a fortified peak within Garstang Palace on Thanedd Island, was constructed in the 1240s by the Aen Seidhe using magic to lift heavy stone. The tower featured a portal connecting it to Tor Zireael and gained notoriety among mages due to its unpredictable effects when utilized. Following the Brotherhood of Sorcerers' discovery of the portal, they attempted to block it to prevent further chaos. On July 1st, 1267, a coup led by Ciri's uncontrolled use of the portal resulted in a magical explosion that destroyed the tower and ejected Ciri into the Korath desert.
