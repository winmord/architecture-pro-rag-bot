White Frost refers to an impending event foretold by Ithlinne, which is believed by some to be caused by changes in Earth's orbit and axis. It is also associated with the arrival of an ice age that will freeze the northern hemisphere around 3,000 years after Nimue's life. According to prophecy, this cataclysmic event cannot be prevented, even with powerful magic, but may be halted through the opening of Elder Blood portals to another world. In Norse mythology, White Frost is analogous to Fimbulwinter, a catastrophic winter that precedes Ragnarök, suggesting a shared theme of apocalyptic consequences.
