The 'Conjunction of the Spheres' is a historically significant book from CDPR's witcher games, detailing a cosmic event that occurred 1500 years ago during which numerous creatures from parallel universes were accidentally introduced into our reality. These include ghouls and graveirs, often referred to as relics of the Conjunction by elves who claim humans arrived in this world around the same time they mastered magic, contradicting their own belief that humans are newcomers from the Conjunction. The book is found on numerous bookshelves across all three Witcher games and can be purchased or randomly looted. In 'The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt', it's available as random loot in various houses around Toussaint, while in 'Wild Hunt' expansion Blood and Wine, it can be found in Silverton's warehouse by the town hall. Despite its complex title, the Conjunction of the Spheres is explained in simple terms: when two distant vessels (worlds) collide on a cosmic sea, some crew members from one ship may move to another, and cargo can spill onto neighboring ships. This event led to the arrival of monsters like ghouls and basilisks, while humans learned the arcane mysteries of magic unbeknownst to them beforehand. The book also hints at scholars' tendency to describe simple phenomena in overly complex terms.
