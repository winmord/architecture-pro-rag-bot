Geralt of Rivia is a mutated human trained from childhood to hunt monsters as a witcher, possessing superhuman reflexes and strength. He was born into a family of witchers in 1211, making him significantly younger than Yennefer, who was born in 1173. Geralt's complex personality is characterized by his troubled past, gruff demeanor, and deep sense of goodwill, which often leads to him being fiercely independent and non-conformist. He has significant relationships with characters like Dandelion and Yennefer, and he is driven by a desire to protect those he cares about from supernatural threats.

As a skilled swordsman and monster hunter, Geralt participates in notable events such as foiling an assassination attempt on King Foltest and joining a Scoia'tael uprising. He has also completed contracts for various clients, including the mysterious Man of Glass in the Blood and Wine expansion.

Geralt's world is one of morality grayness, where witchers like himself are rare beacons of hope. His character process likely differs from others, granting him the ability to feel emotions and empathy. In various media, including games, TV shows, and films, Geralt has been portrayed as a dueling warrior class hero who excels at combat and possesses utility skills.

Notable adaptations include The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, a Netflix series, the Blood and Wine expansion, and Gwent: The Witcher Card Game. Geralt's name has been translated into multiple languages, reflecting his significance in various cultures. He has also appeared as a guest character in other games, such as Soulcalibur VI and Monster Hunter: World.
