Yennefer von Fürstenburg is a complex and significant character in the Witcher series, known for her exceptional magical abilities and tumultuous personality. Born into a pathological family with mixed human-elven bloodline, Yennefer was emotionally abused by her father and rejected by her mother, leading to an attempt at suicide as a student at Aretuza's first class. Despite this traumatic start, she excelled in her studies under the guidance of Archmistress Tissaia de Vries and became a highly talented sorceress after undergoing magical corrections for her deformities.

Yennefer's relationship with Geralt of Rivia is complex and central to the story, spanning across multiple games and media appearances. In The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings, she becomes involved in Geralt's quest against the Nilfgaardian Empire and provides crucial magical assistance, while in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, her storyline intertwines deeply with Geralt's as they search for Ciri, his adopted daughter. Yennefer is also a key character in several comic book series, including Curse of Crows and Killing Monsters.

Physically, Yennefer has curly black hair, pale skin, and piercing violet eyes, and is described as having a slender build. Her beauty is enhanced by her magical abilities, making her one of the most striking characters in the Witcher series. With her complex personality reflecting her tumultuous past, Yennefer is intelligent, determined, and resourceful, often helping Geralt on his adventures.

Yennefer's character has been portrayed by actress Anya Chalotra in Netflix's The Witcher TV series (2019-present).
