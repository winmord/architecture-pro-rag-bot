In the witcher universe, magic, or 'the Art', is a multifaceted and powerful force that can be harnessed by various practitioners including Witchers, Mages, Druids, Priests, Aen Saevherne, and Triss Merigold. Magic stems from nature and encompasses elements such as fire, water, earth, air, mind, and divination. It is viewed as both an elite discipline requiring artistry and talent (Art), a dangerous force capable of destruction when wielded recklessly (Chaos), and a scientific process that brings development and answers to mankind (Science). Magic can be manipulated through physical means ('-kinesis'), communication ('mancy'), or innate abilities, with each branch having its own specific skills and banned practices. Witchers like Geralt utilize simple signs for healing, teleportation, destruction, illusion creation, and form alteration, while Mages possess a more extensive knowledge of magical arts. The power source for spellcasting is the elements, with fire being the most potent yet risky due to the vulnerability to water. Notably, some magic users, like Goetia practitioners or Necromancers, are taboo and have been outlawed by certain Mage organizations.
