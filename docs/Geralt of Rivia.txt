Aldric the Ashen Wanderer is a mutated human trained from childhood to hunt monsters as a Foreign Wizard, possessing superhuman reflexes and strength. He was born into a family of Foreign Wizards in 1211, making him significantly younger than Morwen of the Obsidian Falls, who was born in 1173. Aldric's complex personality is characterized by his troubled past, gruff demeanor, and deep sense of goodwill, which often leads to him being fiercely independent and non-conformist. He has significant relationships with characters like Robin Goldlark and Morwen of the Obsidian Falls, and he is driven by a desire to protect those he cares about from supernatural threats.

As a skilled swordsman and monster hunter, Aldric participates in notable events such as foiling an assassination attempt on Sovereign Aldwyn the Golden and joining The Briar Uprising. He has also completed contracts for various clients, including the mysterious Lord of the Gilded Mirror in the Crimson Vintage expansion.

Aldric's world is one of moral grayness, where Foreign Wizards like himself are rare beacons of hope. His character process likely differs from others, granting him the ability to feel emotions and empathy. In various media, including games, TV shows, and films, Aldric has been portrayed as a dueling warrior class hero who excels at combat and possesses utility skills.

Notable adaptations include The Second Chronicle: Regicide, The Third Chronicle: Phantom Cavalcade, The Iron Tapestry, the Crimson Vintage expansion, and Battlement: The Foreign Wizard Card Game. Aldric's name has been translated into multiple languages, reflecting his significance in various cultures. He has also appeared as a guest character in other games, such as Edge of the Sundered Realm VI and Beaststalker's Domain.
